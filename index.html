Ending Food Exploitation.
Starting with the Source.

Every year, 1.3 billion tonnes of food is lost or wasted worldwide (FAO, 2019) — even as 733 million people face hunger (FAO SOFA, 2023). In India, post-harvest losses alone cost ₹1.53 lakh crore annually (ICAR, 2022). FoodWatch exposes the root causes, tracks government action, and drives real rural solutions.

The Scale of the Crisis

These numbers represent real lives, real land, and real resources squandered every single day.

1.3B Tonnes of food lost/wasted per year — FAO, SOFA 2019
733M People facing hunger globally — FAO, SOFA 2023
40% Of India's food waste occurs at farm & post-harvest stage — NITI Aayog, 2018
₹1.53L Cr Annual post-harvest losses in India — ICAR, 2022
8–10% Of global GHG emissions from food loss & waste — IPCC AR6, 2022
70% Of India's food loss occurs in rural/farm areas — MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23

Sources: FAO State of Food & Agriculture (SOFA) 2019 & 2023 · ICAR Post-Harvest Technology Division 2022 · NITI Aayog Discussion Paper 2018 · IPCC Sixth Assessment Report 2022 · MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23
