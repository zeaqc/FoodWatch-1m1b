Government Initiatives

Central and state governments are deploying schemes, investments, and legislation to tackle food loss. All scheme data below is sourced from official ministry portals, annual reports, and Union Budget documents — not secondary estimates.

India: Central Government Schemes

Key Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI), Ministry of Agriculture, and other central schemes targeting food loss reduction.

🏭 MoFPI

PM Kisan Sampada Yojana (PMKSY)

A flagship scheme to create mega food parks, agro-processing clusters, cold chain & value-addition infrastructure. Under the Union Budget 2023–24, the scheme outlay was revised to ₹4,600 crore for the 2021–26 period. As of March 2023, 41 mega food parks have been approved and 24 operationalised (MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23).

Status: Active  |  Budget (2021–26): ₹4,600 Cr  |  Source: MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23
🥦 MoFPI

Operation Greens

Provides 50% subsidy on transport costs from surplus to deficit regions and 50% subsidy on cold storage hiring for TOP crops (Tomatoes, Onions, Potatoes). Expanded to 22 perishable crops in 2020 after COVID-19. Budget allocation: ₹500 crore (Union Budget 2018–19 launch; continued under PMKSY envelope). As of 2022–23, over 4,800 interventions have been funded (MoFPI portal data).

Status: Active  |  Subsidy: 50% transport & storage  |  Source: MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23
🌾 MoA&FW

PMFBY — Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Comprehensive crop insurance covering sowing to post-harvest losses from weather, pests, and disasters. Union Budget 2023–24 allocated ₹13,625 crore. As of Kharif 2022, 5.6 crore farmer applications were covered with ₹1,28,847 crore in claims paid since inception (MoA&FW Progress Report, March 2023). Post-harvest coverage (up to 14 days after harvest) directly reduces distress dumping risk.

Budget 2023–24: ₹13,625 Cr  |  Claims paid (total): ₹1,28,847 Cr  |  Source: MoA&FW PMFBY Dashboard
📱 MoA&FW

eNAM — National Agriculture Market

Pan-India electronic trading portal linking APMCs for real-time price discovery. As of March 2023: 1,361 mandis in 23 states/UTs are live on eNAM, with 1.76 crore farmers and 2.51 lakh traders registered. Total trade value: ₹2.36 lakh crore since inception (eNAM Portal, MoA&FW, March 2023). Reduces middleman exploitation through price transparency.

Live Mandis: 1,361  |  Registered Farmers: 1.76 Cr  |  Source: eNAM Portal, MoA&FW, March 2023
🏗️ MoFPI

PLI Scheme for Food Processing

Production-Linked Incentive scheme offering 10–15% incentive on incremental sales for food processing companies. Total outlay: ₹10,900 crore (2021–2027). As of 2022–23, 176 applicants approved with projected incremental sales of ₹33,494 crore and employment for 2.5 lakh persons (MoFPI PLI Dashboard, 2023). Reduces raw produce waste via value-addition.

Outlay: ₹10,900 Cr  |  Approved applicants: 176  |  Source: MoFPI PLI Dashboard 2023
🌱 MoA&FW

Agri Infrastructure Fund (AIF)

₹1 lakh crore financing facility for post-harvest infrastructure — silos, pack houses, primary processing units, cold stores. Provides 3% interest subvention and CGTMSE credit guarantee. As of March 2023, ₹40,407 crore sanctioned across 67,226 projects (AIF Portal, DAC&FW). Average project size: ₹60 lakh — accessible to FPOs and small processors.

Total Fund: ₹1,00,000 Cr  |  Sanctioned (Mar 2023): ₹40,407 Cr / 67,226 projects  |  Source: AIF Portal DAC&FW
🍚 DFPD

National Food Security Act (NFSA) & PMGKAY

Legal entitlement to 5 kg subsidised grain/person/month for 81.35 crore beneficiaries (67% of population). Extended under PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY) as free grain from Jan 2023. The DFPD Annual Report 2022–23 documents total grain lifted: 540.28 lakh MT in 2022–23, helping FCI clear storage and reduce spoilage from overcrowding.

Beneficiaries: 81.35 Cr  |  Grain lifted 2022–23: 540.28 lakh MT  |  Source: DFPD Annual Report 2022–23
🌡️ MoFPI / NABARD

Integrated Cold Chain Infrastructure Scheme

Provides 35% grant (50% for NE/hilly regions) for integrated cold chain projects from farm gate to retail. As of 2022–23, 291 projects sanctioned with total cold chain infrastructure capacity created: 709.96 lakh MT-km (MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23). Minimum project size requirement ensures rural hubs are viable.

Grant: 35–50%  |  Projects sanctioned: 291  |  Source: MoFPI Annual Report 2022–23

Rural-Focused Government Schemes

These schemes specifically target rural food loss — at the village, block, and district level. Many remain under-utilised. All data from official ministry portals and annual reports.

🌾 MoA&FW

RKVY-RAFTAAR

Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana — Remunerative Approaches for Agriculture and Allied sector Rejuvenation. Provides flexible block grants to states for agriculture and allied sectors, including post-harvest infrastructure at village level. Budget 2023–24 allocation: ₹7,150 crore. States can use RKVY funds for rural pack houses, primary processing centres, and custom hiring centres for post-harvest equipment (MoA&FW Annual Report 2022–23).

Budget 2023–24: ₹7,150 Cr  |  Source: MoA&FW Annual Report 2022–23; Union Budget 2023–24
🧅 MoA&FW

PM-AASHA — Price Assurance & Farm Services

Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan — a price support scheme that triggers government procurement when market prices fall below MSP by more than 10%. Directly prevents distress dumping events. As of 2022–23, 42 lakh MT of pulses and oilseeds procured under PM-AASHA when prices crashed, protecting farmers in MP, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra (CACP Annual Report 2022–23).

Procurement trigger: >10% below MSP  |  Source: CACP Annual Report 2022–23; DAC&FW data
🏘️ MoRD / NRLM

DAY-NRLM — Rural Livelihoods Mission

Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission mobilises rural women into Self-Help Groups (SHGs) for economic activities including food processing, preservation, and marketing. As of March 2023, 9.14 crore women in 83.57 lakh SHGs are active (MoRD Annual Report 2022–23). SHGs supported under NRLM can establish village-level pickle, jam, and drying units — converting surplus produce into shelf-stable products and preventing waste.

Active SHGs: 83.57 lakh  |  Women covered: 9.14 Cr  |  Source: MoRD Annual Report 2022–23
🏗️ MoRD

Grameen Bhandaran Yojana (Village Grain Storage)

Provides 15–25% capital subsidy for construction of rural storage godowns (50–10,000 MT capacity) by farmers, FPOs, and cooperative societies. Facilitates warehouse receipt financing — farmers can borrow against stored grain instead of selling at harvest-time low prices. As of 2022, over 47,000 rural storage units have been funded under this and allied NABARD schemes (NABARD Grameen Bhandaran Status 2022).

Subsidy: 15–25% capital cost  |  Units funded: 47,000+  |  Source: NABARD Grameen Bhandaran Status 2022
🌱 MoA&FW / SFAC

Farmer Producer Organisations (FPO) Scheme

The government is promoting 10,000 new FPOs with ₹6,865 crore budget (2019–24). Each FPO receives ₹15 lakh handholding grant for 3 years, credit guarantee, and equity support. FPOs can collectively invest in village-level cold rooms, grading equipment, and direct market linkages — eliminating intermediaries. As of March 2023, 3,231 FPOs registered under this scheme (SFAC/MoA&FW portal 2023).

Target: 10,000 FPOs  |  Budget: ₹6,865 Cr  |  FPOs registered: 3,231 (Mar 2023)  |  Source: SFAC/MoA&FW 2023
🌾 MoTA / WFP

Grain Bank Scheme (Tribal Areas)

Ministry of Tribal Affairs runs community grain banks in tribal regions to provide food security buffer stock and reduce distress selling at harvest. WFP India supports expansion of the model. As of 2022, around 2,000 grain banks are operational in 12 tribal-concentration states — but cover less than 4% of estimated need (MoTA Grain Bank Status Report 2022). Scale-up is critically underfunded.

Operational grain banks: ~2,000  |  Coverage of need: <4%  |  Source: MoTA Grain Bank Status Report 2022
🚜 MoA&FW

Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanisation (SMAM)

Provides 40–50% subsidy on post-harvest machinery including threshers, cleaners, dryers, and storage structures for individual farmers. SC/ST farmers and NE states get 50% subsidy. Critically, custom hiring centres (CHCs) funded under SMAM allow farmers to rent equipment they cannot afford to own. As of 2022–23, over 18,000 CHCs established nationwide (SMAM Annual Progress Report 2022–23).

Subsidy: 40–50%  |  CHCs established: 18,000+  |  Source: SMAM Progress Report 2022–23; MoA&FW
☀️ MNRE / PM-KUSUM

PM-KUSUM — Solar for Rural Cold Chain

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan provides 60% subsidy on solar pumps and off-grid solar power for farmers. Component C of PM-KUSUM supports solarisation of grid-connected agriculture pumps. Critically, solar power enables off-grid cold rooms in villages without reliable electricity. Budget 2023–24 allocation: ₹4,000 crore; over 3 lakh solar pumps installed under components A & B as of March 2023 (MNRE Annual Report 2022–23).

Budget 2023–24: ₹4,000 Cr  |  Solar pumps installed: 3 lakh+  |  Source: MNRE Annual Report 2022–23

Policy Timeline: India's Journey

Key legislative and policy milestones in India's battle against food loss and waste, with official source citations.

  • 2013

    National Food Security Act Enacted

    Legal right to food for 67% of India's population; mandated FCI grain procurement at MSP, creating a floor price safety net for farmers.

  • 2014

    Food Safety and Standards (Packaging) Regulations

    Standardised food packaging requirements to reduce spoilage; introduced date-marking standards to curb premature discard.

  • 2016

    Solid Waste Management Rules

    Mandated large food waste generators (hotels, wedding halls, markets) to process food waste at source via composting or biogas.

  • 2017

    eNAM Expansion to 470 Mandis

    National Agriculture Market platform scaled up, directly attacking middleman exploitation through price transparency.

  • 2018

    Operation Greens Launched

    Dedicated scheme for TOP crops providing transport and storage subsidies to prevent price-crash waste events.

  • 2020

    Agri Infrastructure Fund (₹1 Lakh Crore)

    Massive post-harvest infrastructure investment fund announced as part of Atma Nirbhar Bharat package.

  • 2021

    PLI Scheme for Food Processing

    ₹10,900 Cr incentive scheme to boost processed food exports and reduce raw produce waste through value-addition.

  • 2022–2024

    PM Vishwakarma & ODOP Schemes

    One District One Product (ODOP) initiative and artisan support schemes helping local food processors add value and reduce raw waste.

Global Policy Frameworks

International commitments and frameworks that India and other nations are working within.

🌏 UN

SDG 12.3 — Halve Food Waste by 2030

United Nations Sustainable Development Goal targets halving per-capita food waste at retail and consumer levels and reducing food losses along production and supply chains by 2030.

🌿 FAO

Save Food Initiative

FAO's global partnership involving governments, businesses, and civil society to reduce food loss and waste across the entire supply chain through technical assistance and capacity building.

🤝 G20

G20 Food Security Working Group

India's 2023 G20 Presidency put food security and sustainable agriculture at centre stage. The G20 MACS declaration endorsed technology-driven post-harvest loss reduction commitments.
